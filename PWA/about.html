About Knoledge Search

For Schools and businesses.

Description

Knoledge Search is a search engine designed for educational and business use. It helps users find information across a wide range of topics on Wikipedia.

The site is in testing and includes installable web and desktop app versions.

Our mission

We believe every good question is the beginning of something worth discovering. We are committed to making knowledge accessible to everyone. Knoledge Search uses our custom built algorithm to provide the most relevant results using SSAI (Safe Search Algorithm Initiation). This locks the search to provide relevant results; this cannot be modified for different users, but certain approved links will be viewable in newer versions.

What you can do

Search across Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo, and Wikidata with highlighted query matches. (v0.1.7)
